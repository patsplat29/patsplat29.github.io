I've been animating for almost a year and have been throwing everything into it — frame-by-frame characters, object show episodes, weird little Flash games. If it moves and makes people smile, I want to make it.

I have a deep love for the Flash animation era and try to keep that style alive through my own games and animations. Object Oddballs is where I get to push my storytelling and character design the furthest.
