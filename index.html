01 — About


I've been animating for almost a year now and have been throwing everything into it. Whether it's frame-by-frame characters, object show episodes, or weird little Flash games — if it moves and makes people smile, I want to make it.

I have a deep love for the Flash animation era and work to keep that style alive through my own games and animations. My object show, Object Oddballs, is where I get to push my storytelling and character design the furthest.
